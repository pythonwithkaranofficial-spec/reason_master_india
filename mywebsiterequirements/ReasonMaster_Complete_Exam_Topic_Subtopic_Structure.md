## 1. Taxonomy Architecture

```text
EXAM CATEGORY
    ↓
EXAM
    ↓
EXAM → TOPIC PRIORITY / RELEVANCE
    ↓
MASTER REASONING TOPIC
    ↓
SUBTOPIC
    ↓
WORKED EXAMPLES / PRACTICE
    ↓
MCQ QUESTION BANK
```

The same master topic is reused across multiple examinations. Exams are a mapping layer over the shared reasoning curriculum.

```json
{
  "questionText": "...",
  "options": ["...", "...", "...", "..."],
  "correctIndex": 0,
  "difficulty": "easy | medium | hard",
  "hint": "...",
  "explanation": "...",
  "examTags": ["..."]

```

# 17. Important Reconstruction Rule

Do not duplicate the curriculum for every examination.

```text

IBPS PO → Analogy
SBI PO → Analogy
```

as three separate topic entities.

Correct:

```text
MASTER TOPIC
└── Analogy
      ├── SSC CGL mapping
      ├── IBPS PO mapping
      ├── SBI PO mapping
      ├── Railway mappings
      ├── Defence mappings
      └── Police mappings
```

This permits one question bank and one curriculum to serve many examinations.
